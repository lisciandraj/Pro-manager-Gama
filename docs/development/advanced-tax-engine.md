# Exact taxes and fiscal positions

The authenticated `coco_tax_engine` RPC supplies configuration, quote and purchase previews, invoice confirmation and internal advance previews. Development retains the server-verified active administrator gate. Configuration changes also require the established accounting permissions and MFA; private definitions, fiscal substitutions, snapshots and advance allocations cannot be written directly by authenticated or service roles.

Named taxes support a percentage or a fixed amount per physical unit, included prices, an explicit sequence, and adding a component to subsequent tax bases. Included prices are solved algebraically. Financial discounts and rewards reduce the percentage base while their `tax_quantity=0` preserves fixed taxes on the merchandise. Their entered amounts are before tax even when the source merchandise has included taxes. Cent residuals preserve included prices without inventing an effective rate or a negative component.

Documents choose rounding per line or for the whole document. Global calculations allocate residual cents deterministically, and every allocated line and component sums to the document totals. Existing documents without named taxes retain their legacy percentage arithmetic and line rounding. A missing `tax_ids` uses the legacy percentage; an empty array means explicitly exempt.

Fiscal positions substitute or remove named taxes and substitute accounts. Explicit document selection takes precedence over a contact assignment; an active automatic country rule applies next, ordered by priority and ID, with separate sale/purchase scopes. Base accounts follow the product/category/company hierarchy before fiscal substitution. A draft quote retains whether its position was explicitly selected; the calculation records the resolved position.

Quotes store their exact calculation and product-line snapshots. Acceptance carries these into the order. A new invoice resolves effective rules on its issue date, compares them with the accepted financial terms, and returns `TAX_RULES_CHANGED` with both calculations when confirmation is necessary. The invoice dialog shows the old and current totals and requires an explicit confirmation tied to the current calculation. Repeating a creation command retains its actor-bound receipt and does not recalculate a previously created draft using subsequently changed master data.

Supplier invoices and accounting expense drafts use the same calculation before insertion/posting. Product/category accounts, fiscal substitutions and each tax account are frozen before the financial writer runs. Issued pieces, payments, credits, early-payment corrections and reversals retain their original accounts and tax IDs after a master change or archival.

Internal advances apportion the accepted components to the requested net advance. Dedicated private buckets retain each order line and its component amounts. Partial final invoices release those frozen buckets cumulatively; the last allocation consumes every remaining cent. Fixed and compounded components remain separate, advances credit the configured liability, and deductions debit it. Merchandise quantities, advance allocations, dated revenue recognition and cancellation protection retain their established lifecycles.

## Snapshot contract

`private.coco_tax_compute(side, partner, date, lines, fiscal_position, rounding)` returns `schema: coco.tax.v1`, `subtotal`, `tax`, `total`, `rounding`, `side`, `date`, `fiscal_position_id`, `lines` and aggregated `taxes`. Side accepts `sale`/`customer` and `purchase`/`supplier`.

Each line has a stable `key`, quantity, entered price, discount, exact net unit price, allocated subtotal/tax/total, base account, tax quantity and base factor, frozen `rules` and calculated `taxes`. Component identity is `id`; `account_id`, `code`, `name`, `calculation_type`, `rate`, `fixed_amount`, `base`, `amount` and `tax_category` describe the actual component. Trusted advance allocations use `frozen_amount`/`frozen_base` in their rules to preserve the captured currency amounts. They do not rewrite the tax master.

`private.coco_tax_recompute_snapshot(calculation, factor)` recomputes an issued invoice from frozen rules, retaining full revenue while reducing percentage tax bases. Fixed physical taxes and their contribution to later percentage bases remain unchanged. For allocated advance components, the engine rebuilds this fixed share from ordered frozen rules; the captured rounding offset preserves the exact issued amount at factor one. `private.coco_tax_scale_snapshot(calculation, fraction, account_override)` allocates captured currency components, including fixed taxes, for advances and proportional corrections. It preserves component identities and captures `source_line_id`.

## Tax reports and fiscal emission

The tax view includes the actual frozen component bases and amounts with a CSV export. Its report follows ledger dates, reversals and persisted credit/early-payment adjustments. Master changes do not change historical report classifications. `tax_category` explicitly distinguishes VAT from other levies; a new fixed levy defaults to other taxes. Form 104 remains an unsubmitted draft and separates other levies from its VAT totals.

The existing SRI provider schema supports one of its permitted EC VAT rates per line. Fiscal preparation rejects an unsupported fixed, compound, multiple, advance or deduction mapping with `SRI_TAX_MAPPING_REQUIRED`. It also rejects an exact frozen base that cannot be represented using the provider's unit-price precision with `SRI_TAX_PRICE_PRECISION_REQUIRED`. The internal invoice, account posting, component report and PDF remain usable. No synthetic effective VAT percentage or unverified statutory code is sent to a provider. Adding a statutory mapping requires the actual provider schema and legal component codes.
