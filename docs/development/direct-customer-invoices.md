# Customer invoices created in Accounting

Open **Accounting → Revenue → Customer invoices without an order**. An accountant with full accounting visibility can create a draft with products or free descriptions, quantities, prices, discounts, named tax rules or a legacy percentage, fiscal position, rounding method and payment terms. The server calculates every amount. A saved draft receives Coco's internal document reference and history; it contributes no revenue, receivable, recovery candidate or SRI selection until posting.

Posting requires accounting validation rights and the current draft revision. If tax rules or account mappings changed after saving, the accountant must review and save the draft again. The posted invoice retains its customer, company, currency, terms, line bases, tax components and accounts. It uses the existing `external_invoices` and `external_invoice_lines` registry, journal, receivables, payments, bank matching, reminders and finance reports. Its `order_id` is null: posting creates no quote, sales order, delivery, reservation or stock movement.

The invoice detail provides payments, PDF, early payment discount terms, gift card/wallet settlement and the existing SRI process. Early discount terms can be selected at creation, including reducing the percentage-tax base at issue. Actual cash, the earned discount and its tax correction remain separate accounting records. Optional line period dates create the existing revenue recognition schedules. Each schedule consumes its actual frozen income account; independent supplier allocations can split across several expense source lines, retaining the original commercial allocation key and exact total cents.

The payment link action preserves the same PDF as the invoice download, with its SHA-256, a frozen customer-facing projection and an expiring, revocable capability. It uses migration `20261108171337_invoice_payment_links.sql` and the existing checkout store. Invoice links require posted invoices with an outstanding balance; they cannot sign a quote or create an order. Tokens are displayed once and are not stored in module history. The payment processing extension owns provider configuration, verified payment evidence, current dated balances and canonical receipt allocation. Development previews retain the server's active-administrator gate.

Credits allocate a percentage across the original frozen income and tax components, including credits after payment. Repeated partial credits absorb the original components' final cents and taxable bases. An earned early payment discount is proportionally restored inside the credit, using its frozen expense and tax components; VAT and cash are never refunded twice. The notes are canonical accounting adjustments and can be cancelled through Accounting's adjustment workflow.

Open **Refund** beside a posted credit to record an actual bank/cash refund. Credit first clears unpaid debt. Refundable cash is capped by the credit's paid portion, actual received cash and the remaining signed receivable credit, less prior cash and wallet refunds. Gift value returns through its original wallet. Migration `20261108173029_direct_invoice_paid_credits_refunds.sql` extends the existing `return_refunds` source with an exclusive financial-adjustment origin; it creates no physical return. The refund debits the original receivable account and credits its frozen bank account. It changes no revenue or tax a second time. Native partial bank matching consumes its negative cash amount and adds no duplicate movement.

An active cash or wallet refund prevents credit cancellation. Matched cash refunds must be unmatched before cancellation; cancelling a refund retains its source and reverses its journal. A payment funding an active refund or an early-discount compensation cannot be withdrawn before its dependent credits/refunds. Invoice cancellation requires reversing active payments, credits, withholdings, schedules and other settlements first. All source corrections retain module history, customer/invoice references and the original accounts.

These are internal management documents until the existing fiscal process issues them. Coco's `FAC` reference is independent of Ecuador's legal sequence. The SRI preparation process accepts its supported exact percentage-tax lines and frozen identity; combinations without a legal tax mapping (fixed, compound, multiple components or a reduced tax base) raise `SRI_TAX_MAPPING_REQUIRED`. No authorization is manufactured.

Migration `20261108163504_standalone_customer_invoices.sql` adds the origin/customer fields, line storage, guards, checked RPC and financial projections. Historical order invoices retain their order references and ledger entries. Their canonical customer backfill is schema metadata maintenance; it does not replay numbering, accounting or business history.
